# Personal identity and website — There’s always more to notice

September 27, 2026. Approved brand and website design baseline; implemented locally, not publicly deployed.

Jason approved the new branding, specifically praising the JF integration and knife-edge terminals. The new direction supersedes the original mockup; original mockups remain historical references.

## Creative roles

The user requested an art director, agentic Allan Peters specializing in logo and brandmark design, a cartoonist–fine artist chief advisor, and a poetic copywriter. These are AI creative roles, not actual participation by or endorsement from the named designer. The art director has overall visual responsibility. The identity designer owns the mark, the advisor critiques visual character and small-size structure, and the copywriter develops an original voice. No external professionals were contacted or hired.

## Direction

The company site offers a studio's clear promise. The personal site opens with a person's attention: “There’s always more to notice.” A literary serif, cocoa ink, cream paper, clay and lavender establish an intimate editorial pace. Biography appears before software. Running, gardens, cars, music and wide-ranging questions are treated as parts of life, not credentials or productivity techniques.

The new material develops the existing “Practice of Paying Attention” mockup. It keeps the documented personal facts and avoids invented anecdotes, portraiture or testimonials. The AI-generated still life is explicitly symbolic illustration. The technical work retains its documented release distinctions.

## Identity

“The Turn” is the approved personal signature: a single F/J silhouette with angular upper strokes and a curved lower hook. The advisory review favored a compact one-color shape and rejected hobby-icon collage, generic eye/leaf symbolism and decorative details needed to explain the mark. The brand board includes primary, reverse and small-size specimens. Jason accepted this identity on September 27, 2026. Preserve the approved silhouette and typography relationship in subsequent work.

## Editorial discipline

The copywriter brings conversational rhythm, grounded detail and a little dry humor. The site speaks in Jason's voice and does not imitate a named performer or poet. Personal claims are grounded in the previous brief. The confirmed Orca contact email is used here as the existing direct route; no LinkedIn or personal domain is invented.

## Implementation and review

- `site/`: responsive semantic HTML and CSS; core experience requires no JavaScript.
- `branding/personal-v1/`: editable SVG marks, favicon, identity board and usage notes.
- `artwork/`: generated original plus optimized JPEG; exact prompt and provenance saved in README.
- `scripts/`: dependency-free local build, server and reference validation.
- `mockups/`: previous direction preserved unchanged.

Run `npm run dev` and open http://127.0.0.1:4175. Run `npm run check` to build and validate local destinations, anchors and image alternatives. Build output is a standalone `dist/` directory.
