01The person

There’s a person on the other side of every useful thing.

A life with a few
open tabs.

I like making things. Software nearly became my first career; it stayed with me, and what began as a side pursuit has grown into a serious body of work.

I follow questions into things people can use—from personal applications to tools for other developers. I care about how a system works, how someone experiences it, and the choices that connect the two.

My background as an attorney shapes how I read a problem: the language, the consequences, and the requirements that need untangling. I like learning how people think. An odd idea in physics or philosophy can keep me occupied, too.

When we work together, I ask careful questions, explain my choices, and carry the work from first idea to working application. You’ll know where things stand, understand the decisions along the way, and have something useful you feel confident making your own.

Good questions have a way of opening things up.
