# Jason Fricano · The turn

Approved personal identity · September 27, 2026. Jason accepted the new branding and specifically endorsed the integrated JF mark and its knife-edge terminals. Preserve that geometry as the identity baseline. AI design role requested as agentic Allan Peters; no claim of actual designer involvement, endorsement, or portfolio imitation. The website art director owns layout, typography, palette application and the total site experience. This identity role owns the mark and its usage. The cartoonist–fine artist chief advisor helped separate expressive editorial artwork from a reproducible identity.

## Three directions considered

1. **Open thought:** paired organic parentheses around a seed. Listening and growth were legible, but the result approached familiar eye / wellness-leaf shorthand. Rejected.
2. **Field stamp:** a notebook or folded page containing personal initials. Easy to understand, but too literal and too dependent on the page metaphor. Rejected.
3. **The turn — selected:** one uninterrupted silhouette. A folded F upper structure turns into a rounded J lower hook. The right edges share a deliberate cut; the lower gesture offers the surprise. The initials reward a second look rather than becoming a diagram of a profession.

This direction places a personal signature on the work without combining legal, software, garden, car and music icons. It remains recognizable in one color. Orca's signal is a company emblem; this is a small signature with a human bend.

## Usage

Keep the mark upright. Minimum digital size 24 px for normal display; favicon at 16 px is an intentional exception. Preserve at least 16 viewBox units of space around the drawn silhouette when next to other content. Do not rotate, add eyes, enclose in a decorative badge, stretch, or combine with Orca's mark. Use either cocoa on paper or paper on cocoa for the primary identity. Clay, lavender and ochre belong in artwork and supporting surfaces; they are not automatically approved small-text colors.

The wordmark is live text reading **Jason Fricano**, not outlined imitation handwriting. The art director selected Helvetica Neue / Arial / sans-serif at weight 600, with −0.04em tracking, to balance the literary Georgia headlines. The board and standalone identity assets follow that choice; the wordmark remains font-dependent.

Palette: cocoa #302B28, paper #F5F0E7, clay #B95C3B, lavender #AFA9CB, ochre #C9A64C. Solid geometry remains crisp; controlled variation belongs in the illustration. Maintain descriptive alternatives when art conveys content, and empty alternatives when the neighboring text already supplies meaning.
