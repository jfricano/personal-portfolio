02 / Approach

Curious at the start. Thorough at the finish.

I use an agentic engineering workflow to move quickly across design, code, and testing. I make the product decisions, review the work, and check that the result does what it says.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Find the real problem.

Who is this for? What would make their day easier? What must be true for the product to be trusted?

02 / BUILD

Work across the whole stack.

Shape the experience, the architecture, and the implementation together, rather than handing the difficult parts off to later.

03 / VERIFY

Make it hold up.

Test the edges, state the limits, and leave people with something they can actually use and understand.

“I nearly became a developer. The building part never went away.”
03 / About Jason

An attorney with a strong developer streak.

I'm happy in my legal career. I've also always loved creating tools—especially when a messy problem can become something clear and genuinely helpful. Software started as a side quest and grew into a serious body of work.

That mix shapes how I build. I ask careful questions, explain the choices, and stay with the details from the first idea to a working application. I founded Orca Solutions as the home for the software I make and publish.
