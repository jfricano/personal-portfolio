03 / About Jason

Paying attention is a practice.

I'm an attorney with a strong developer streak. I almost became a developer; instead, making things stayed beside a legal career I'm happy in. What began as a side quest has grown into a serious body of software work.

I like learning how other people think, exploring odd ideas in physics and philosophy, and turning messy problems into tools that help. The common thread is curiosity paired with care.

When I build, I ask careful questions, explain the choices, and stay with the details from first idea to working application.
